Children’s Bible Book

Birth and Early Life of Jesus for Children


Teaching children about the life of Jesus Christ at an early age helps build strong values of love, kindness, faith, and obedience. Birth and Early Life of Jesus for Children, published by Praise Publishing House, is specially created to help young readers understand the beautiful story of Jesus in a simple, engaging, and chronological way. This book is part of the Chronological Children’s Bible – Gospel Series, Volume 1, focusing on the most important moments from Jesus’ birth and childhood.


The Promise of a Savior

The story begins with God’s promise to send a Savior for the world. Long before Jesus was born, prophets spoke about His coming. In this book, children are introduced to these promises in a gentle and easy-to-understand way, helping them see how God planned everything with love and care. The book explains that Jesus is God’s special gift to all people, sent to bring hope, peace, and salvation.


The Birth of Jesus in Bethlehem

One of the most loved stories in the Bible is the birth of Jesus. Birth and Early Life of Jesus for Children beautifully narrates how Mary and Joseph traveled to Bethlehem, where Jesus was born in a humble manger. The story highlights important moments such as the angels appearing to the shepherds, the bright star in the sky, and the joy of welcoming baby Jesus into the world.
Through warm illustrations and simple language, children learn that Jesus was born in humble surroundings to teach us that love and goodness matter more than wealth or power. The nativity story encourages children to be thankful, kind, and trusting in God’s plan.


Early Visitors and God’s Protection

The book also shares the story of the wise men who followed the star to find Jesus. Children learn how they brought special gifts—gold, frankincense, and myrrh—to honor Him as a King. This part of the story helps children understand respect, generosity, and worship.
The early life of Jesus also includes God’s protection over His family. The book gently explains how God guided Mary and Joseph to keep baby Jesus safe, showing children that God watches over His people at all times.

Jesus Growing in Wisdom and Love

As Jesus grew, He showed obedience, kindness, and wisdom. Birth and Early Life of Jesus for Children shares stories from Jesus’ childhood that highlight His love for God and His parents. One important moment is when young Jesus is found in the temple, listening and asking questions. This story teaches children the value of learning, curiosity, and staying close to God.
The book presents Jesus as a role model for children, encouraging them to be loving, respectful, and eager to learn. By seeing how Jesus lived as a child, young readers can relate to Him and understand how they too can follow God in their daily lives.

Designed Especially for Children

Published by Praise Publishing House, this children’s Bible book is thoughtfully designed for young readers. The chronological storytelling helps children understand the sequence of events clearly, while engaging illustrations keep them interested. The simple language makes it ideal for parents to read aloud, for Sunday school lessons, and for children to enjoy independently.

A Perfect Introduction to the Gospel

Birth and Early Life of Jesus for Children is more than just a Bible storybook—it is a meaningful introduction to the Gospel. It helps children develop faith, compassion, and moral values from a young age. This book makes a wonderful gift for Christian families, churches, and schools looking to nurture children in God’s Word.
