Children’s Bible Book

Jesus Christ Life Story For Children


Introducing children to the life of Jesus Christ is one of the most meaningful ways to help them grow in faith, kindness, and love. Jesus Christ Life Story for Children, published by Praise Publishing House, is specially created to present the story of Jesus in a simple, engaging, and child-friendly way. This book helps young readers understand who Jesus is, why He came into the world, and how His life teaches us to love God and others.


A Gentle Introduction to the Life of Jesus

Children connect deeply with stories, especially when they are told in a way they can easily understand. This book begins with the birth of Jesus, explaining how God sent His Son as a gift of love to the world. The story is shared in clear language, helping children feel close to Jesus and understand His importance from the very beginning.
The life story of Jesus is presented step by step, allowing children to follow His journey as He grows, learns, and shows God’s love through His actions.


Jesus’ Early Life and Childhood

The early life of Jesus teaches valuable lessons for children. In Jesus Christ Life Story for Children, young readers learn how Jesus obeyed His parents, grew in wisdom, and loved God from an early age. Stories such as Jesus being found in the temple help children understand the importance of learning, listening, and staying connected to God.
These moments show children that Jesus understands them and that they too can follow God in their daily lives.


Teaching Love Through Stories and Parables

As children continue reading, they are introduced to Jesus’ teachings in a simple and meaningful way. The book shares key moments from Jesus’ life that highlight love, forgiveness, kindness, and helping others. Children learn that Jesus cared for everyone—young and old, rich and poor—and taught us to love one another.
The storytelling style helps children remember these lessons and apply them at home, school, and with friends.

Illustrated Storytelling for Better Understanding

Published by Praise Publishing House, this children’s life story of Jesus includes engaging illustrations that bring each scene to life. Pictures help children visualize events, understand emotions, and stay interested while reading. Illustrations also support parents and teachers in explaining Bible stories clearly.
This combination of simple text and beautiful artwork makes the book ideal for bedtime reading, Sunday school lessons, and personal devotion time for kids.

Chronological and Child-Friendly Gospel Presentation

The book follows a chronological structure, helping children understand the sequence of Jesus’ life events. From His birth and early years to His teachings and acts of love, each part of the story connects smoothly. This structure builds a strong foundation of faith and understanding in young readers.

Ideal for Families, Churches, and Schools

Jesus Christ Life Story for Children is perfect for Christian families, churches, and educational settings. It encourages parents to teach their children about Jesus in a loving and meaningful way. The book also makes a wonderful gift for birthdays, Christmas, baptisms, and special religious occasions.

A Faith-Filled Book from Praise Publishing House

Praise Publishing House is dedicated to publishing high-quality Christian books that nurture young hearts. This book reflects their commitment to sharing God’s Word with children in a way that is engaging, trustworthy, and spiritually enriching.

Helping Children Walk in the Way of Jesus

The Jesus Christ Life Story for Children is more than a book—it is a guide to building faith, character, and hope. By learning about Jesus’ life, children are encouraged to walk in His ways and grow in love, faith, and obedience.
