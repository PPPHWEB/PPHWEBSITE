Children’s Bible Book

Nativity Story Book For Children


The story of Jesus’ birth is one of the most loved and meaningful stories in the Bible. A Nativity Story Book for Children helps young readers understand this beautiful event in a simple, warm, and engaging way. Published by Praise Publishing House, this children’s nativity book is thoughtfully created to introduce kids to the true meaning of Christmas and God’s great gift to the world—Jesus Christ.


Introducing the Nativity Story to Young Hearts

Children are naturally curious and love stories filled with wonder. The nativity story, with angels, stars, shepherds, and a newborn King, captures their imagination while teaching powerful lessons of love, humility, and faith. This nativity story book presents the birth of Jesus in easy-to-understand language so children can clearly grasp the message behind the story.
Through gentle storytelling, children learn how Mary and Joseph trusted God, how Jesus was born in Bethlehem, and how God chose simple and humble surroundings to bring His Son into the world.


Beautiful Illustrations That Tell the Story

A Nativity Story Book for Children becomes even more meaningful when paired with illustrations. Each scene—from the angel’s message to Mary, to the peaceful manger where Jesus was born—is brought to life with colorful and expressive artwork. These illustrations help children visualize the story and stay engaged while reading.
Pictures also make it easier for parents and teachers to explain the nativity story. Children can connect emotions to the characters and remember the story more clearly, making Bible time enjoyable and interactive.


A Chronological and Faithful Retelling

Published by Praise Publishing House, this nativity story book follows a chronological and faithful retelling of Jesus’ birth. The book gently guides children through the events leading up to the nativity, including the journey to Bethlehem, the birth of Jesus, and the joyful announcement to the shepherds.
The story also includes the visit of the wise men, teaching children about worship, generosity, and honoring Jesus. By presenting the nativity story in order, children can easily understand how each part connects to God’s bigger plan.

Simple Language for Young Readers

This nativity story book is written especially for children, using clear and age-appropriate language. Complex ideas are explained simply, making the story accessible to young readers and listeners. Whether read aloud by parents at bedtime or shared during Sunday school lessons, the book helps children understand the message of Jesus’ birth without confusion.

Teaching Values Through the Nativity Story

The nativity story teaches important values that children can apply in their daily lives. Through the story of Jesus’ birth, children learn about humility, obedience, kindness, and trusting God. They understand that God’s love is for everyone and that even small acts of faith can have great meaning.

Perfect for Christmas and Beyond

While the nativity story is often shared during Christmas, this book is meaningful all year round. It is ideal for family reading, church programs, Christian schools, and as a thoughtful gift during Christmas, baptisms, or special occasions.

A Trusted Children’s Christian Book from Praise Publishing House

Praise Publishing House is committed to creating high-quality Christian books for children that nurture faith and understanding. This nativity story book reflects that mission by offering engaging storytelling, beautiful illustrations, and a strong biblical foundation.

Helping Children Discover the Joy of Jesus’ Birth

A Nativity Story Book for Children is more than a story—it is an invitation for children to discover the joy, hope, and love found in the birth of Jesus Christ. This book helps plant seeds of faith that can grow throughout a child’s life.
