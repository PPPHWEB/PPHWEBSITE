Who We Are

We are Praise Publishing House—
not just a ministry, not just a publisher, but a movement of the heart, rooted in God’s Word and blooming through creativity.

This journey didn’t begin with a business plan or a platform. It began with a burden—a holy ache—to help people, especially the next generation, encounter the Bible in a way they’d never forget.

We are...
  • * Pastors, shepherding souls and walking with people through joy and sorrow
  • * Artists, crafting stories and illustrations that carry truth into everyday life
  • * Parents, praying that our children and yours will grow up wrapped in the promises of Scripture

But above all…
We are worshippers.

Every page we design, every verse we include, every reflection we write is our offering— a quiet expression of love, faith, and surrender to the One who gave us the Word in the first place.

Our deepest hope is that through these books, you’ll encounter the Creator.
Not just in knowledge… but in wonder. In comfort. In beauty.
That you’ll feel seen. Known. Invited closer.

Because to us, this isn’t publishing.
It’s a Ministry.


It’s Worship.
And it’s for you.

Welcome to a place where devotion becomes art—and art becomes worship.

Our Mission


To inspire people of all ages to Engage with the Bible through creative, beautiful, and spiritually rich resources that Transform Hearts, Renew Homes, and Revive Communities.

Our Vision


To raise a generation that Doesn’t Just Read the Bible—but Remembers it,
Reflects on it,
Colors it,
Loves it,
and Lives it..
