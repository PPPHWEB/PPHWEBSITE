Who We Are

We are...
  • Pastors, shepherding souls and walking with people through joy and sorrow

Our deepest hope is that through these books, you’ll encounter the Creator.
Not just in knowledge… but in wonder. In comfort. In beauty.
That you’ll feel seen. Known. Invited closer.

Because to us, this isn’t publishing.
It’s a Ministry.

It’s Worship.

And it’s for you.

Our Core Beliefs

At Praise Publishing House, our work is not driven by trends, but by truth. It flows from a deep, unwavering belief that the Word Of God Still Transforms Lives—and that creativity, when surrendered to Him, becomes worship.

Here’s What We Hold Close to Our Hearts:

We Believe the Bible is the Living Voice of God.


Every word of Scripture is God-Breathed—eternal, unshakable, and always relevant. It’s not just a book—it’s a Lifeline, a compass, a place where the weary find rest, and the seeking find answers. We don’t just want people to read it. We want them to Encounter it, experience it, and carry it with them.


We Believe Creativity is A Sacred Echo Of the Creator.


God is the Original Artist—the one who painted the sunrise and spoke galaxies into being. We believe He placed creativity inside us not as a distraction, but as a Divine Invitation—to reflect His beauty, to express His truth, and to worship in ways that touch both heart and soul. When we color a verse, journal a prayer, or tell a story soaked in Scripture, We Are Worshipping.


We Believe Families Grow Stronger Around the Word.


When a parent reads the Bible with a child, when a teen reflects on a Psalm with pencils in hand, when a mother prays scripture over her unborn baby—
Faith Takes Root. We believe that the home is holy ground, and when Scripture is honored there,Legacy is Born.


We Believe the Gospel is For Everyone — Every Age, Every Heart, Every Home.


Whether you’re young or old, curious or committed, hurting or hopeful— There’s A Place For You At the Table Of Grace. That’s why we create resources that are gentle yet powerful, simple yet profound—designed for all kinds of learners, in all seasons of life. Because the Good News isn’t meant to be locked in theology books…
It’s meant to be Held, Colored, Read Aloud, and Lived.

Restoring the Legacy of Sir William James Wanless

Some stories are too precious to be forgotten.

In 1889, Dr. William James Wanless came to Western India with little more than faith and a willingness to serve. On July 4, 1894, the medical work in Miraj began. From that humble start grew a mission of healing, hope, education, and Christian witness that reached Miraj, Sangli, Kolhapur, and countless villages beyond.

Lives were changed. Communities were lifted. A quiet flame of compassion was lit that still warms this region today.

We believe a legacy like this deserves to be remembered and honored. Through Praise Publishing House, we are preserving this history in research, writing, and publication. Through Beth El Foundation, we are carrying that same spirit of service into communities with practical care and love.

The work he began was never really about one man. It was about the goodness of God shown through willing hands. That story continues, and by God's grace, we are honored to help carry it forward.

If this story stirs something in your heart, we invite you to walk with us. Follow our work, share this legacy with others, and pray for the mission that continues in Miraj today. Together we can make sure that a life given in service is never forgotten, but remembered, honoured, and lived out again in our own time.

To God be the glory.
