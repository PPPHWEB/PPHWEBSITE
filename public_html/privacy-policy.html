Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 July 2026

1. Introduction

Praise Publishing House ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when you use praisepublishinghouse.com.

2. Information We Collect

  • Personal details you provide: name, email address, phone number, and shipping/billing address when you place an order, subscribe, or contact us.
  • Order information: the products you purchase and your transaction history.
  • Payment information: processed securely by our payment partners (Razorpay and PayPal). We do not store your card or banking details.
  • Technical & usage data: IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, collected via cookies and analytics tools.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders and send order confirmations.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your enquiries.
  • To send updates, newsletters, or offers (only if you have opted in).
  • To improve our website, products, and services.
  • To detect and prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.

4. Payment Information

All payments are handled by Razorpay (India) and PayPal (international) on their PCI-DSS compliant platforms. Your card and banking data are entered directly on the gateway and are never stored on our servers. We only receive a confirmation of payment status and limited transaction details needed to fulfil your order.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Payment gateways (Razorpay, PayPal) to process transactions.
  • Shipping and logistics partners to deliver physical orders.
  • Service providers such as analytics and email tools that help us operate the website.
  • Authorities where required by law.

6. Cookies & Analytics

We use cookies and tools such as Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site and to improve your experience. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

7. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections, to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent. Purchases must be made by an adult.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to other sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties and encourage you to review their policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
